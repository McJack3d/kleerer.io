compare.

The independent supplement comparator — score the label, price the active. Every product gets an open-source Health & Compo Score computed from its label and public proofs, and a price per gram of actual active, not per kilo of powder. No sponsors, no affiliate links.

>

Not medical advice. Scores reflect label composition and public transparency only — not whether a supplement is right for you, and not independent lab verification of batch content. Dietary supplements are not a substitute for a varied diet. Talk to a healthcare professional before supplementing, especially if you are pregnant, on medication, or have a medical condition.

Prices are EU list prices, snapshot July 2026 — several brands (Myprotein, Bulk, Prozis) run structural promotions, so effective prices can be 30–45% lower. Data confidence is flagged per product. Found an error? The dataset and scoring script are open — please fix us.
