compare.

The independent supplement comparator — score the label, price the active. Every product gets an evidence-based Health & Compo Score, a price per gram of actual active, and a dosage check against your needs — and my stack sums whatever you combine, catching doses that quietly cross an EU limit. Behind it: a proprietary price-and-label archive, recorded daily and growing — history no one can recreate after the fact. No sponsors, no affiliate links.

no profile — every product shows its dose, none is checked against you optional — personalises the "dosage vs your needs" line on every product. Weight and age are required; height only sharpens the vitamin D target (via BMI). Healthy adults; evidence-based ranges, EU upper limits respected; not medical advice.

Not medical advice. Scores reflect label composition and public transparency only — not whether a supplement is right for you, and not independent lab verification of batch content. Dietary supplements are not a substitute for a varied diet. Talk to a healthcare professional before supplementing, especially if you are pregnant, on medication, or have a medical condition.

Prices are EU list prices, snapshot July 2026 — several brands (Myprotein, Bulk, Prozis) run structural promotions, so effective prices can be 30–45% lower. Data confidence is flagged per product. Found an error? The dataset and scoring script are open — please fix us.
